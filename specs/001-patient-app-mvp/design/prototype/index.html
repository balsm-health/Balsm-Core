How Balsm works

Regulated by Egypt PDPC

Your health profile stays on this phone

Allergies, medications, conditions, contacts — all stored on your device. We never see them. If you lose your phone, your health profile is gone with it (unless you sign in on a new device using your email/Google/Apple — then we restore from your encrypted backup).

What we do see

Only your account info: email, name, country, language, and date of birth (encrypted on our server, so even our staff can't read it without an audited reason).

If you share an emergency QR

The QR carries a copy of your card encrypted with a key only the QR holds. Anyone scanning it sees your card. You can revoke it any time.

If you decide to leave

Settings → Delete account → 7 days to change your mind → everything gone. No "hidden retention", no marketing list.
